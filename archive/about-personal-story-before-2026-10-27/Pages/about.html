WHAT RIDEWIRE BMX IS ABOUT

BMX STORIES.
SCENE FIRST.

RideWire BMX is a one-person BMX media project built around the riders, places and moments that make the scene matter.

The site brings together BMX news, rider spotlights, throwback films and scene history. Freestyle is our main focus, with BMX Racing covered too—from major contests and results to the local places and communities behind them.

We look beyond the scoreboards to tell the stories around the riding: the people who build a scene, the videos that stay with us and the parks that shape generations of riders.

RideWire is growing story by story. Where we use outside reporting or photography, we aim to credit the source and link back so readers can follow the trail.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE SITE

ONE CREATOR.
ONE EDITOR.

I’m Karl, the sole creator and editor of RideWire BMX. The idea comes from a lifetime of memories made around the ramps and the people I met there.

As a kid, I took to the ramps at Parque da Juventude in Portimão in 2005, around the time of BMX Summer Comp. I met Peter Ellinger, Pedro Dias, Pedro Seca, Ruben “Trolha” Antonio, Nuno Aguas, Texas, Zen, Filipe Sebastião, Silveira and many more. Some of those early skatepark friendships became friendships for life.

BMX had a prominent, energetic scene in Portugal at the time. Around Portimão, people knew me as “Dip”—and some knew me by the longer nickname “Dipshit.” Those names still take me back to the same close-knit crew and the days spent riding together.

That personal connection is why the history of Parque da Juventude matters to me. RideWire is a place to follow what is happening in BMX now and to keep the people, parks and moments that shaped the scene in view.
