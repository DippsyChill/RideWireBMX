What cookies and similar technologies may be used when you visit.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies can store information on your device or read information from it. Some are used to make a service work; others may support embedded media, measurement or advertising.

RideWireBMX’s current site

The RideWireBMX frontend does not currently set its own analytics or advertising cookies, use local storage for visitor tracking, or provide a cookie-preference tool. The live hosting configuration has not yet been confirmed.

Third-party content

Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts and media or embeds from Instagram, YouTube, Pinkbike and Flickr. These third parties may use cookies or similar technologies and receive technical information from your browser when their content loads. Their use is governed by their own policies. External links only contact those services if you follow them.

Your choices

You can manage or clear cookies through your browser settings. Blocking third-party content may affect how embedded videos, social posts, photos or fonts appear. Browser controls do not necessarily prevent a service from receiving basic connection information when a page requests its content.

The Irish Data Protection Commission explains the rules in its guidance on cookies and other tracking technologies. This preview does not yet include a consent-management tool; the site’s third-party embeds and hosting setup should be reviewed before public launch.
