BMX STREET / EVENT RECAP · 22 AUG 2026

STREET SERIES.
TWO CITIES.

Simple Session moved BMX into the streets of Tallinn and Riga for a two-country summer series.

← Back to all news

Simple Session took its 2026 BMX Street Series out of the arena and into two Baltic capitals. More than 30 riders from 18 countries were announced for the Tallinn and Riga dates, with the Riga stop’s MVP award going to Argentina’s Gaspar Guendulain.

A format built for the streets

The series ran in Tallinn on 19–20 August before moving to Riga for 22–23 August. Organisers positioned it as a focused street-riding edition, mixing purpose-built obstacles with city riding and a festival atmosphere. Garrett Reynolds, Reed Stark, Billy Perry, Sasha Cambon and Joe Jarvis were among the international names announced ahead of the series.

That format gave the event a different rhythm from a conventional single-venue contest: the competition spectacle and the city itself became part of the riding experience.

Riga recognises Guendulain

At the Riga stop, Guendulain was named MVP. Ernests Šulcs received the Best Latvian title, while Casey Starling received a special prize. The organisers’ recap described a rain-affected day, adding another challenge to the closing stage.

The 2026 series was also a practical experiment in taking a well-known BMX event brand across borders and into local street-culture festivals. Its success will make the next format decision one to watch.
