BMX FREESTYLE / PORTUGAL | 8 OCT 2026

PORTUGAL RIDES
AGAIN.

A national circuit returns in Torres Vedras. In Portimao, Parque da Juventude's BMX story spans three decades.

← Back to all news

Portugal's BMX Freestyle national circuit returned on 3 October after a three-year pause. Around 25 riders competed at the remodeled Torres Vedras skatepark, where current national champion Diogo Santos took the win.

A circuit back on the calendar

The Torres Vedras round brought amateur and professional riders together on a refreshed course. Santos, who rides for Caldas da Rainha, carried his national champion form into the return event. A regular series gives riders more chances to compete at home and creates a route into the discipline for developing athletes.

A Portimao park with its own BMX timeline

We have expanded this history into a separate feature on the park, the missing-ramp years and the riders who carried BMX into the streets. Read the full Portimao BMX scene story →

For RideWire's editor, Parque da Juventude was a local riding spot. Its story shows how BMX spaces evolve, from the original race track and wooden ramps to event-built facilities and a new concrete street-and-bowl park.

From race track to Rebel Jam, then a new park

The municipality's 2015 BMX dossier dates the track's inauguration to 1996, calling it one of Portugal's oldest. In 2010, Portimao hosted Rebel Jam; DIG's retrospective notes the event left a brand-new park behind for local riders.

In 2019, a redesigned 400-metre BMX Race course opened following a project led by Clube Bicross de Portimao. Redevelopment began in October 2023; BMX Race athletes retained access to the race track while other parts of the grounds were closed.

The wider park reopened on 26 April 2025 after around 18 months of work. Its radical-sports area introduced a concrete street course and bowl inspired by Fortaleza de Santa Catarina; the former skate-ramp area was adapted for futsal and 3x3 basketball. The race track and concrete park are separate facilities within the wider park.
