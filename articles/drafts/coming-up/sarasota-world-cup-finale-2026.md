# Sarasota set to decide the 2026 BMX Racing World Cup

**Standfirst:** The final two rounds of the UCI BMX Racing World Cup arrive in Sarasota this weekend, with the season titles still to be decided.

The 2026 UCI BMX Racing World Cup reaches its final stop in Sarasota, Florida, on 31 October and 1 November. Rounds 9 and 10 close a season that began in Europe, made its first visit to China, and now returns to the United States for its finale.

Sarasota is the last opportunity for riders to add points to their overall totals. Two races remain, so the weekend can still reshape the standings across the Elite and Under-23 categories. The UCI confirms the dates and venue, but the final start lists and detailed session schedule should be checked against the event's latest updates before publication.

The preceding rounds in Chongli produced four different Elite winners. Australia’s Joshua Jolly and Switzerland’s Cédric Butti shared the Men Elite victories, while Great Britain’s Bethany Shriever and the Netherlands’ Laura Smulders won in Women Elite. Smulders’ Sunday victory was her 31st World Cup round win, extending her record in the series.

That close contest sets up a meaningful finale: riders arrive with different kinds of momentum, and every remaining result counts toward the season outcome. The Chinese rounds also marked a first for the series, taking the World Cup to Mentougou and Chongli in October.

The UCI calendar lists Sarasota for rounds 9 and 10 on 31 October and 1 November. RideWire will follow the finals and update this report with confirmed results and the overall winners once racing is complete.
