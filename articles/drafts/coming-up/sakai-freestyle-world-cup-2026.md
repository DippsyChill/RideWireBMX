# Park and Flatland return to Sakai for the World Cup finale

**Standfirst:** The final 2026 UCI BMX Freestyle World Cup stop brings Park and Flatland riders to Sakai, with Elite contests in both disciplines.

Sakai, Japan, hosts the final stop of the 2026 UCI BMX Freestyle World Cup from 26 to 29 November. The UCI’s current event listing includes Park and Flatland, with Men Elite and Women Elite contests in each discipline.

It brings two very different forms of freestyle BMX together at the season’s closing round. Park riders link tricks across ramps and obstacles, balancing technical difficulty, height and flow. Flatland riders build combinations on level ground, using balance, bike control and timing. Sakai gives fans a chance to follow both disciplines over one late-season weekend.

The World Cup has already visited Montpellier, Birmingham and Shanghai in 2026. Sakai is the final round listed on the UCI calendar, so it will close the international series and give riders one last opportunity to make their mark in the season rankings.

The UCI event page confirms the Japanese venue, dates and four Elite categories. A full daily timetable and rider field were not available in the listing checked on 27 October, so RideWire should confirm those details with the organizer closer to the event.

We’ll be watching for the final results, standout runs and how the closing round shapes the series picture across Park and Flatland.
