Nitro Circus 2.0 is set for Moreeb Dune in Abu Dhabi on 19–20 December.

Nitro Circus 2.0 will bring two days of action to Liwa Festival in Abu Dhabi on 19 and 20 December 2026. The announced location is Liwa Village at Moreeb Dune, in the Liwa Oasis.

A desert setting for the tour

The event announcement promises BMX and Freestyle Motocross athletes, large tricks, pyrotechnics and Nitro Circus stunts at one of the UAE’s best-known desert destinations. The venue makes this stop visually distinct from the tour’s usual stadium and arena format.

Ryan “R-Willy” Williams is included among the BMX athletes presented by Nitro Circus. The published schedule lists afternoon and evening showtimes on 19 December and an afternoon show on 20 December, all at Liwa Village.

What fans should know

Tickets were announced as on sale on 9 October. Nitro Circus describes the production as “2.0”; further details about the full rider line-up and exact show format may be released closer to the date. We’ll update this report if the schedule or athlete roster changes.

Event details and the announced BMX content are based on Nitro Circus’ own news post and schedule.
