WORLD CUP / EVENT RECAP · 21 OCT 2026

SHANGHAI
GOES BIG.

FISE’s open West Bund festival paired BMX Freestyle World Cup competition with a city-wide celebration of urban sport.

← Back to all news

The 2026 FISE World Series Shanghai finished on 18 October after four days on the West Bund. A recap published by the Shanghai municipal government reported 400,000 visits to the festival, which brought BMX Freestyle and other action sports into a large, free-entry public event.

A festival built around the waterfront

The event covered 180,000 square metres and included 18 competitions across five disciplines: BMX Freestyle, skateboarding, breaking, scooter and inline skating. This year’s open format connected the competition area with the surrounding waterfront and public spaces.

For BMX fans, the headline contests were the UCI World Cup rounds in Park and Flatland. The wider festival setting also put the riding in front of visitors who might not normally attend a standalone competition.

Sun Sibei takes the women’s Park win

China’s Sun Sibei won the women’s Park final on home ground. Sun Jiaqi finished second to make it a Chinese one-two, with Australia’s Natalya Diehm taking third. The local report described the result as a highlight for the home crowd.

Shanghai’s event points to the growing scale of urban sports festivals: elite competition sharing space with public activities, music and a riverside setting. The 2026 edition drew BMX into the centre of that mix.
