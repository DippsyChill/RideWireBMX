DEW TOUR BMX / HISTORICAL RECAP · 21 JUN 2015

ENARSON
TAKES IT.

A rewind to the 2015 Chicago BMX Streetstyle podium and the riders who filled the field.

On 21 June 2015, Dennis Enarson won Dew Tour BMX Streetstyle in Chicago. Garrett Reynolds finished second and Tyler Fernengel third in a 16-rider field. This is a historical archive feature, not a report of a current Dew Tour BMX event.

A familiar trio at the top

The podium gathered three riders who were central to the street scene of the era. Enarson claimed the win, Reynolds followed in second, and Fernengel rounded out the top three. Chad Kerley finished fourth, with Kevin Peraza fifth and Dakota Roche sixth.

Those names show the depth of the contest: the top six included riders whose street parts and contest runs helped define the period. Chad Kerley also won the Dew Tour BMX Street Best Session the previous day, with Enarson second.

Why revisit this stop?

Dew Tour results remain a useful snapshot of how BMX street contests were staged and who was pushing the discipline at the time. For riders who followed the series, the Chicago stop is a compact time capsule of a stacked field; for newer fans, it is a quick route into the event history.
