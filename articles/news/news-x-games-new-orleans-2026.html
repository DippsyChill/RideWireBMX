X GAMES / NEW ORLEANS · 26 JUL 2026

NEW ORLEANS.
BIG MOMENTS.

Garrett Reynolds extended his street record, while the Park and Dirt finals delivered close podiums at the Superdome.

← Back to all news

X Games New Orleans ran 24–26 July 2026 at the Caesars Superdome. BMX delivered an all-time milestone in Street, a tight Men’s Park podium and a Dirt win for Brady Baker.

Reynolds adds a 19th X Games gold

Garrett Reynolds won Men’s BMX Street for the 19th X Games gold medal of his career, extending his record for the most gold medals in X Games history. Tom Smillie took silver and Boyd Hilder bronze. Reynolds has now medalled in 24 of 26 BMX Street appearances, according to the event report.

Three riders separated by 3.6 points in Park

Justin Dowell won Men’s BMX Park with a best score of 93.6. Kieran Reilly followed at 92.8 and Marcus Christopher finished third with 90.0. It was a compact top three where every run mattered.

In Dirt, Brady Baker topped the podium on 96, ahead of Mike Varga and Ryan Williams. The official results make for a weekend that balanced a career milestone with a new generation pushing hard across Park and Dirt.
