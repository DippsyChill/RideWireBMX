RIYADH WORLDS
CALLED OFF.

The 2026 Urban Cycling World Championships will not take place, removing a major Park and Flatland date from the BMX calendar.

The 2026 UCI Urban Cycling World Championships in Riyadh have been cancelled, the UCI and Saudi Cycling Federation confirmed on 16 October. Scheduled for 3–7 November, the event was to award world titles in BMX Freestyle Park and Flatland, as well as trials.

A major date disappears from the calendar

The announcement means riders, teams and fans will not make the planned trip to Riyadh this November. For BMX Freestyle, the event was one of the season’s biggest stages: the world championship titles and rainbow jerseys were due to be decided in both Park and Flatland.

The UCI said the decision followed a joint assessment of recent developments in the region and their effect on the logistics of staging the championships. Its notice does not announce a replacement host city or new date.

What happens next?

The cancellation carries sporting consequences beyond the lost event weekend. The UCI has since said the 2025 world champions retain their titles until the eve of the 2027 Urban Cycling Worlds. It is also reviewing how the cancellation affects the BMX Park qualification pathway for Los Angeles 2028.

That leaves the international scene waiting for revised qualification details and the next update on the championship calendar. We’ll follow the UCI’s announcements as they arrive.
