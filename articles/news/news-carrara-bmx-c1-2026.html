Danelutti and Diehm take Elite wins by the same 1.67-point margin at the Gold Coast event.

← Back to all news

Alec Danelutti and Natalya Diehm both won their Elite BMX Freestyle Park finals by 1.67 points at the AusCycling National Freestyle Centre in Carrara on 27 September.

Danelutti returns to the top

Danelutti scored 95.67 to win the men’s final, edging Samual Grace on 94.00. Anar Aliev completed the podium with 92.67. AusCycling noted that Danelutti had returned from injury to take the victory.

Diehm leads the women’s field

Olympic medallist Natalya Diehm won the women’s final on 93.67, with Sarah Nicki just behind at 92.00. Alirah Norris finished third on 89.00.

The closest finish of the day came in Amateur Boys, where Rhylan Seymour beat Felix Stokes by 1.33 points. The event also included junior age categories at the Gold Coast venue.
