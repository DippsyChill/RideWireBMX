UCI WORLD CUP / FISE · 19 MAY 2026

JAPAN TAKES
MONTPELLIER.

Park and Flatland gold for Japan made for a commanding BMX Freestyle World Cup week at FISE Montpellier.

← Back to all news

Japan’s BMX Freestyle team left Montpellier with all four UCI World Cup titles on offer. At the five-day FISE festival, Rimu Nakamura won men’s Park and Rena Shirai, aged 14, won the women’s final. Japanese riders also topped both Flatland podiums.

Nakamura leads the men’s Park final

Nakamura took the men’s Park win ahead of Justin Dowell and Marcus Christopher. The podium paired the Japanese rider’s winning run with two established US competitors, while Argentina’s José Torres Gil finished fourth. Great Britain’s Reef Way and Dylan Hessey also reached the top ten.

The depth of the field made the result notable: the final brought together international World Cup regulars, with a broad mix of styles and approaches to the Montpellier course.

Shirai makes her mark at 14

In the women’s Park final, Shirai claimed gold at just 14. The result was part of a broader Japanese sweep: Chiaki Todaka won women’s Flatland and Kyo Hayakawa won men’s Flatland. Four titles across the two BMX disciplines gave Japan a clear headline at this round.

FISE Montpellier ran from 13 to 17 May, pairing the UCI competitions with the festival’s wider action-sports programme. The next stop for the riders is the rest of the World Cup season, where the Montpellier results set an early benchmark.
