EXTREME BARCELONA · 6 SEP 2026

BARCELONA
LEVELS UP.

A new UCI Hors Class status brought added weight to the BMX Park finals at Parc del Fòrum.

Extreme Barcelona’s BMX Park competition stepped up from UCI C1 to Hors Class in 2026, drawing an international field to the city’s Parc del Fòrum from 4–6 September.

Reilly takes the men’s final

Great Britain’s Kieran Reilly claimed the men’s win with 90.33 points. Dylan Hessey finished second on 86.09, and Australia’s Samual Grace took third with 84.07.

Molnár adds another win

Noémi Molnár carried her European Championship form into Barcelona, winning the women’s final on 83.07 points. Sarah Nicki was second on 81.67 and Japan’s Rena Shirai third on 74.74.

The upgraded Hors Class status made the weekend a valuable stop in the international Park calendar, with leading riders from Europe, Australia and Japan all on the podium.
