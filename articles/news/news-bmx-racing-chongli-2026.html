UCI WORLD CUP · 12 OCT 2026

CHONGLI
ON THE LINE.

Four different Elite winners share the spotlight as the BMX Racing World Cup reaches its final stretch in China.

The BMX Racing World Cup’s first visit to Chongli, China, delivered four different Elite winners across rounds seven and eight on 10–11 October.

Jolly and Butti split the men’s wins

Australia’s Joshua Jolly won Saturday’s Men Elite final in 36.436 seconds, ahead of Colombia’s Juan Velásquez Carmona and the Netherlands’ Casper Pipers. The podium was Pipers’ first at Elite World Cup level.

On Sunday, Switzerland swept the first four places. Cédric Butti won in 37.350 seconds, with Mark Lüthi second and Filib Steiner third.

Shriever and Smulders trade victories

Great Britain’s Bethany Shriever took the women’s Saturday final, while the Netherlands’ Laura Smulders won Sunday’s round for her 31st UCI BMX Racing World Cup victory.

In Men Under 23, France’s Pierre Geisse won both days. His Sunday finish was especially close: he crossed just 0.001 seconds ahead of compatriot Clément Rocherieux.

Season finale in Sarasota

The series now heads to Sarasota, Florida, for rounds nine and ten on 31 October and 1 November, where the overall 2026 titles will be decided.
