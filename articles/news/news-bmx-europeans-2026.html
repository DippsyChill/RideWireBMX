Noémi Molnár and Jordan Clark are the 2026 UEC BMX Freestyle Park European champions after finals in Darmstadt, Germany, on 23 August.

Molnár takes the women’s title

Hungary’s Molnár found her best run when it mattered. After scoring 49.80 in her first attempt, she put down a 93.00 second run to take gold. Great Britain’s Kitty Joseph finished second on 89.84, with Germany’s Lillyana Seidler third on 86.12.

Clark wins a stacked men’s final

Jordan Clark put himself in front with a 94.00-point first run and held on for the European title. Arsenii Liubishkin scored 93.06 for silver, while reigning world champion Anthony Jeanjean took bronze on 91.80.

The event brought 57 athletes from 18 countries to Darmstadt. Home rider Lillyana Seidler’s bronze was a highlight for the host crowd.
