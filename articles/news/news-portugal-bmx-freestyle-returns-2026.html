Portugal's BMX Freestyle national circuit returned on 3 October after a three-year pause. Around 25 riders competed at the remodeled Torres Vedras skatepark, where current national champion Diogo Santos took the win.

A circuit back on the calendar

The Torres Vedras round brought amateur and professional riders together on a refreshed course. Santos, who rides for Caldas da Rainha, carried his national champion form into the return event. A regular series gives riders more chances to compete at home and creates a route into the discipline for developing athletes.
