BMX RACING / PORTUGAL CUP | 19 OCT 2026

PORTIMAO TOP
THE TABLE.

Clube Bicross de Portimao close the eight-round cup season as team champions, with Iris Moraru retaining her individual title.

Clube Bicross de Portimao finished the 2026 Portugal BMX Race Cup as team champion after the final two rounds in Setubal. The club topped the season table with 164 points, ahead of Team BMX Quarteira on 153 and Nucleo Bicross de Setubal on 115.

A season-long team win

The final rounds took place on 18 October at Parque Verde da Bela Vista in Setubal, closing an eight-round national cup. Portimao's five round victories helped build the points lead that carried the Algarve club to the team title. The standings reward consistent scoring over the season, rather than a single final-day result.

Moraru retains her title

Iris Moraru won the Women +15 overall classification and retained the title she earned in 2025. Gustavo Pereira won the Sub-15 class, while Carlos Rosado took the Cruiser classification. Leonardo Carmo claimed the Men Elite title.
