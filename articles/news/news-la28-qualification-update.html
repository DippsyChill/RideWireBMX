OLYMPIC QUALIFICATION · 19 OCT 2026

THE ROAD TO
LA28 SHIFTS.

The cancelled Worlds had been the first points opportunity for BMX Park riders seeking Olympic qualification.

The UCI says it is working with the International Olympic Committee to adjust the BMX Freestyle Park qualification pathway for Los Angeles 2028 after the 2026 Urban Cycling World Championships were cancelled.

Riyadh was due to open the points race

The November championships had been scheduled as the first chance for Park riders to earn Olympic qualification points. With the event no longer taking place, the original route needs to account for a missing competition on the road to LA28.

The UCI’s 19 October update says qualification will still conclude with the Olympic Qualifier Series: three events planned for May and June 2028. The governing body said it was in contact with the IOC and would share details of the revised pathway in November.

World titles carry over

Because the 2026 titles will not be contested, the 2025 UCI World Champions keep their titles until the eve of the 2027 Urban Cycling World Championships, under UCI regulations.

The announcement gives riders a broad outline, but the revised points route is the key detail still to come. Once the UCI publishes the full pathway, competitors will be able to see which events will shape their qualification campaign.
