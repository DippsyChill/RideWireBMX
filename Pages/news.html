BMX FREESTYLE / EUROPEANS • 23 AUG 2026

Molnár and Clark take European Park titles

Noémi Molnár won the women’s final with a 93-point second run, while Great Britain’s Jordan Clark claimed the men’s title on 94 points in Darmstadt.

Kitty Joseph and Lillyana Seidler joined Molnár on the women’s podium; Arsenii Liubishkin and Anthony Jeanjean took the men’s silver and bronze.

Shanghai puts BMX at the heart of a huge urban sports weekend

OLYMPIC QUALIFICATION • 19 OCT 2026

UCI to revise BMX Park’s LA28 qualifying route

Riyadh was due to provide the first opportunity for BMX Freestyle Park riders to earn points toward Los Angeles 2028. With the Worlds cancelled, the UCI says it is working with the IOC on an adjusted qualification pathway.

The UCI says the route still concludes with three Olympic Qualifier Series events in May and June 2028. Further details are expected in November. The 2025 world champions will retain their titles through to the 2027 Urban Worlds.

WORLD CHAMPIONSHIPS • 16 OCT 2026

Riyadh Urban Worlds will not go ahead

The UCI and Saudi Cycling Federation have confirmed that the 2026 UCI Urban Cycling World Championships, scheduled for 3–7 November, are cancelled. The programme was due to include BMX Freestyle Park and Flatland alongside trials.

The UCI said the decision followed an assessment of recent regional developments and their impact on the event’s logistics. No replacement venue or date has been announced in the cancellation notice.
