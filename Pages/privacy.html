This notice describes the current RideWireBMX website, which is a static editorial frontend. It has no visitor accounts, comment system, contact form, newsletter signup, purchases or first-party analytics.

Information generated when you visit

The web host and security services used to deliver the site may process technical request information such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested page, referring page, and the date and time of a request. This can be used to deliver the site, keep it secure and investigate faults. RideWireBMX does not currently control the host’s exact log retention settings; these details should be confirmed with the hosting provider before launch.

Third-party services and embedded content

Pages load some resources from external providers, including Google Fonts and media or embeds from Instagram, YouTube, Pinkbike and Flickr. When your browser requests that content, the provider may receive connection and device information and may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Those providers handle information under their own privacy notices. Links to other websites also take you outside RideWireBMX.

How long information is kept

RideWireBMX does not currently keep a visitor profile or analytics dataset. Technical server logs, if generated, are managed according to the hosting and security services configured for the live website.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of personal data, and to request portability. You can also raise a concern with the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Contact and updates

A direct privacy contact address and the legal identity of the site operator have not yet been added to this development site. They should be completed, along with hosting details, before the website is publicly launched. This notice will be updated when the site’s services or data practices change.
